# SEO Strategy — Natalie Micale Schultz

Owner: dev + Natalie · Last review: launch prep · Living document.

Not a keyword-stuffing plan. Natalie is a boutique strategic consultant — the goal is to **be findable by warm referrals and serious founder searches**, then convert them. We optimize for the search terms real prospects use, not head-term volumes we can't win.

---

## 1. Positioning → keyword universe

**Who she is (for search intent):**
- Brand strategist / growth strategist
- Advisor to founder-led and growth-stage companies
- Based in Omaha, NE — serves clients nationally
- Not a marketing agency, not a fractional CMO, not a coach
- Named framework: **White Space Growth Framework**
- Credentials: Tory Burch 2023 Fellow · former TV journalist · built and sold a boutique agency (Sentral Holdings, 2025)

**Search intents we optimize for:**

| Intent | Example queries | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Branded** — someone got the referral | "natalie schultz", "natalie micale schultz", "nataliemicaleschultz.com" | Must-win |
| **Category / role** — looking for this kind of help | "brand strategy consultant", "brand strategist for founders", "positioning consultant" | High |
| **Local trust signal** | "brand strategist Omaha", "marketing strategy consultant Nebraska" | High (regional credibility) |
| **Problem-aware long-tail** | "how to reposition a business", "brand strategy for founder-led company", "positioning sprint" | Medium (blog/FAQ-driven) |
| **Named offer** | "strategic compass sprint", "white space growth framework" | Owned terms — protect |
| **Anti-match** ("we do NOT do this") | "fractional CMO Omaha", "marketing agency Omaha" | Do not target — she's not this |

**Head terms we will not chase:** *"brand strategy"*, *"marketing consultant"* on their own. Too broad, dominated by McKinsey/Interbrand/HubSpot content. We win on **founder-led + inflection point + Omaha + named framework**.

## 3. Site-wide technical SEO

### 3.1 Crawlability

This is a client-rendered Vite SPA. Meta updates in the browser via `Seo.tsx`. Google renders JS but slower and less reliably than static HTML.

**Required before launch:**
- [ ] **Prerender public routes** with `vite-plugin-ssg` or `react-snap` — generates static HTML per route so bots see meta + H1 without executing JS. This is the single biggest SEO win for a React SPA.
- [ ] Confirm `sitemap.xml` is emitted at `/sitemap.xml` and includes: `/`, `/about`, `/services`, `/services/strategic-compass-sprint`, `/services/focused-growth-retainer`, `/services/strategic-repositioning`, `/faq`, `/start-the-conversation`. Exclude `/results` until content is real. Exclude legal shells until dated and reviewed.
- [ ] `robots.txt` explicitly points to sitemap (already does).
- [ ] Canonical URL set on every page (already does via `Seo.tsx`).

### 3.2 Structured data

Already implemented (`src/data/seo.ts`). Add on launch:

## 7. What we don't do

- No keyword stuffing in body copy. The voice is editorial; the SEO is architectural.
- No fake reviews, no purchased backlinks, no AI-generated blog dump.
- No "SEO landing pages" that duplicate service pages with city names appended.
- No aggressive interstitials, exit intents, or newsletter modals.

If a decision is between ranking better and staying on brand, brand wins. Ranking follows editorial trust for this type of consultancy — not the other way around.
