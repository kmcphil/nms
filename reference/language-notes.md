## Who Natalie is (use this framing)

- Former agency founder (exited 2025) who understands the founder seat from the inside
- Tory Burch 2023 Fellow
- Strategic growth partner — not freelancer, execution marketer, or agency
- Works at the intersection of brand strategy, positioning, messaging, offer clarity, value ladder, customer insight, AI-enabled systems, and strategic growth planning

**Default positioning sentence:**
> Natalie helps founder-led businesses and growth-stage companies turn unclear stories and scattered momentum into focused growth.

**Alternate / about POV:**
> She helps businesses clarify the story first, then turn that clarity into a concrete strategy and plan for telling that story in the market.

---

## Who the site speaks to

Two audiences — keep both visible; do **not** over-index on “$100K founders” in global copy.

1. **Early-traction founders** — traction exists; priorities/messaging/offers need sharpening; can execute but need direction. (Retainer / ongoing clarity fit.)
2. **Established founder-led / growth-stage companies** — repositioning, research, alignment, premium strategy before a major move. (Premium brand strategy / repositioning fit.)

**Broad homepage language:**
> Brand strategy and growth clarity for founder-led businesses and growth-stage companies at an inflection point.

Reserve narrower revenue bands for offer-specific pages only.

## Voice & tone

**Should feel:** Editorial · founder-led · creative · strategic · clear · confident · premium · human · direct · smart · warm · insightful

**Should not feel:** Generic agency · corporate/cold · influencer · overly playful · academic · execution-heavy · freelancer marketplace

**Themes to reflect:** Strategic clarity · founder-level thinking · focused growth · white space · story before execution · practicality over fluff · premium but approachable · warm confidence · leverage not chaos · brand strategy connected to revenue · human insight over generic AI

## Phrases that earn a place on the site

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| Strategic clarity / focused growth | Full-service marketing |
| Unclear story / scattered momentum | “We do social, email, and ads” |
| Find the white space | Buzzword soup without meaning |
| Story clarity before execution | Execution-first framing |
| Senior-level strategic partner | Freelancer / agency rebuild |
| AI for leverage and clarity | Generic AI productization |
| Founder-led & growth-stage | Only small-business language |
| Start the Conversation / Book a Consultation (qualify) | “Book a free strategy call” |

**Mission (working draft):** Help founder-led and growth-stage companies find the white space, clarify the message, sharpen the offer, and focus on the growth moves that actually matter.

**Vision (working draft):** Trusted strategic partner for founders and leadership teams ready to grow with more clarity, discipline, and leverage — not more noise.

**Core tension the site must solve:**
> Many businesses have momentum, but unclear story, positioning, messaging, or value ladder blocks the next stage of growth. Natalie clarifies the story first, then builds the strategy and plan to tell it in the market.

## Offer language shapes (map to current pages)

### Premium brand strategy / project work
**Promise:** Find the white space, name the market tension, clarify the story, sharpen positioning, turn clarity into messaging + a 60–90 day activation plan.

**Recommended shape:** For businesses that need clearer positioning, messaging, offer architecture, customer insight, and market differentiation before the next major growth move.

**Not for:** Day-to-day marketing execution.

### Growth / focused retainer
**Promise:** Turn scattered marketing, sales, and brand ideas into a focused growth plan; stay close for strategy, decision support, and priority setting — without becoming the execution team.

**What this is not:** CRM / social / email / ads execution, daily PM, website development, “marketing department.”
