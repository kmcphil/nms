# Brand style guide — extracted specs

**Source of truth:** [`style-guide.pdf`](./style-guide.pdf) (Natalie Micale Schultz Brand Guidelines · 2026)

Do not invent alternate brand colors, logo treatments, or logo typography. For website layout pacing and UI chrome, use structural tokens that are **derived only** from black / white / red (opacity tints of `#000000` or `#FFFFFF`) — never a fourth brand color.

---

## Logos

Three official versions only — use files in `public/brand/`, never redraw or retype:

| Version | Use |
|---------|-----|
| Horizontal | Primary · wide formats |
| Stacked | Square / compact spaces |
| NMS monogram | Icon · avatars · stamps |

Formats: AI / SVG for design; PNG for web/social when needed.

### Logo colors (only these)

| Color | Use |
|-------|-----|
| **Black** | Everyday on white / light plain backgrounds; B&W print |
| **White** | On black, dark colors, dark photos, or brand red |
| **Red** (`#C8102E`) | Pop of brand on white / very light backgrounds only — **never** on black, dark, or busy backgrounds |

### Logo font

- **Sunday April** — handwritten script in the logo only
- Some letters are custom (horizontal/stacked: L, T, Z; monogram: N, M, S)
- **Never** use for body/UI text
- **Never** retype the name in this font to invent a logo

### Clear space & minimum sizes (digital)

Leave empty clear space around the logo (no text, graphics, photos, or edges). Scale clear space with the logo.

| Logo | Minimum width (digital) |
|------|-------------------------|
| Horizontal | **160 px** |
| Stacked | **120 px** |
| NMS monogram | **40 px** |

### Do not

- Stretch, distort, rotate, or tilt  
- Add shadows or effects  
- Use off-brand colors  
- Place on busy grounds  
- Retype in another font

## Brand colors (only three)

| Name | HEX | RGB | Usage |
|------|-----|-----|--------|
| Red | `#C8102E` | 200 · 16 · 46 | Main brand color |
| Black | `#000000` | 0 · 0 · 0 | Support |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 · 255 · 255 | Support |

Screens: HEX / RGB. Print: CMYK as in the PDF.

---

## Favicon & profile icon

White NMS monogram on a solid **Red (`#C8102E`)** circle. Even space around the monogram. Use for browser tab and social profiles.

---

## Imagery notes (from brand / brief)

- Photography is selected and curated — not full shoot dumps  
- Prefer light / plain fields behind black logos; use white logos on dark photos  
- Do not place the red logo on dark or busy photography  

---

## Outside this PDF

The PDF does not define website body/UI typefaces, button geometry, or a spacing scale.

- **UI/body fonts** for the site follow the website brief / design direction (not Sunday April): Cormorant Garamond · Newsreader · Outfit  
- **Spacing / buttons / motion** are structural site tokens only — colors remain red / black / white (and opacity tints of black or white)
