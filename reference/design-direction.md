## Positioning

This website should feel like a **boutique strategic consultancy**—not a marketing agency and not a business coach.

Use generous white space, restrained interactions, premium typography, editorial (often asymmetrical) layouts, and a calm visual hierarchy. Prioritize readability and clarity over flashy interactions.

### Core message

Every section should support:

> Businesses don’t have a capability problem. They have a clarity problem.

### Feel

Favor editorial magazine layouts over marketing templates. Folios and hairline rules over card farms. Photography large and purposeful—one primary use per image.

## Feel like

- Stripe Press, Instrument, Pentagram, Collins, Work & Co, IDEO portfolio
- Personal brand with spice — Natalie’s personality, not a firm brochure

## Never feel like

- HubSpot, Deloitte, Accenture, McKinsey
- Generic consulting template, agency brochureware, AI landing pages, card farms
- Marketing-agency flash or coach/lifestyle funnel aesthetics

## Brand SSOT

**[`style-guide.pdf`](./style-guide.pdf)** is the single source of truth for colors, logos, logo colors, Sunday April (logo only), clear space, minimum sizes, and favicon.

See [style-guide-spec.md](./style-guide-spec.md). Tokens live in `src/styles/tokens.css`.

Do **not** invent a parallel palette or logo system.

## Design rules

1. Brand is a hero-level signal — not a nav whisper
2. Photography is large and dominant — one primary use per image (no repeats)
3. Logo script = Sunday April (logo files only). Site type = Cormorant Garamond / Newsreader / Outfit
4. White space is a feature
5. Minimal cards — layouts over boxes
6. Colors only: Red `#C8102E` · Black `#000000` · White `#FFFFFF`
7. One primary CTA per viewport: **Start the Conversation**
8. Build one page at a time — see [build-process.md](./build-process.md)
